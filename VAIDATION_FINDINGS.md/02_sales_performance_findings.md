# Sales Performance Analysis — Findings

## Revenue Concentration by Category (Pareto)
- **Finding:** Roughly the top 17 of 74 product categories account for ~81% of total
  revenue.
- **Interpretation:** Revenue is concentrated but not extremely so — a fairly classic
  Pareto pattern. A meaningful long tail of categories exists but contributes a small
  share of revenue individually.

## Revenue Concentration by Seller
- **Finding:** The top ~17.5% of sellers account for ~80% of revenue.
- **Interpretation:** Seller-side concentration is slightly *tighter* than
  category-side concentration — revenue depends on a relatively small pool of sellers
  more than it depends on a small pool of categories. This matters operationally:
  losing a handful of top sellers would hurt more than losing a handful of top
  categories.
