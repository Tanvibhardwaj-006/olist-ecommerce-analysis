### shipping_limit_date Anomaly (4 records, 1 seller)
- **Issue:** 4 order_items rows (3 orders) have shipping_limit_date values in 
  2020, 15+ months past the dataset's actual order range (Sept 2016–Oct 2018). 
  All 4 rows share the same seller_id, suggesting a seller-specific data error 
  rather than a random/systemic issue.
- **Impact:** Distorted CALENDARAUTO()'s date range in Power BI (max date 
  jumped to 2020-04-09). Resolved by switching to explicit CALENDAR() bounds 
  in the date dimension table instead of relying on auto-detection.
- **Decision:** Retained in the source table as-is (not our data to correct); 
  worked around at the reporting layer via explicit date table bounds.
